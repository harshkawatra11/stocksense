# SKILL: NSE Intraday Trading Specialist

## Core Principles
- NSE market hours: 9:15 AM – 3:30 PM IST
- First 15 minutes (9:15–9:30) are high-volatility — treat opening signals cautiously
- Last 30 minutes (3:00–3:30) see delivery-based selling and position squaring
- Expiry weeks (weekly Thursday, monthly last Thursday) — F&O expiry increases volatility

## High-Probability Intraday Setups on NSE

### Opening Range Breakout
- First 15-min candle establishes range
- BUY if price breaks above range high with volume > 1.5x average
- SELL if price breaks below range low with volume > 1.5x average

### VWAP Reversion
- Stocks below VWAP with RSI < 40 often bounce intraday
- Stocks above VWAP with RSI > 65 often face resistance

### Volume Spike Analysis
- Volume spike > 3x average = institutional activity
- Combined with directional move = strong signal
- Volume spike without price move = accumulation/distribution

## NSE-Specific Patterns

### Nifty Correlation
- Large-cap stocks (>50% weight in index) move with Nifty 50
- Check Nifty direction before taking individual stock signals
- If Nifty futures trending down, avoid BUY signals on banking and IT heavyweights

### FII/DII Daily Data (released at 3:30 PM)
- FII net buying > ₹2000 Cr = bullish for next day
- FII net selling > ₹3000 Cr = bearish near-term
- DII counter-buying FII selling = sideways/stabilization

### Sector Rotation Signals
- Banking stocks lead market moves
- IT stocks move on USD/INR and global tech sentiment
- Pharma stocks often counter-cyclical to broad market

## Stop Loss Rules
- Intraday stop loss: never more than 1.5% below entry
- If stock hits 20-DMA during intraday — strong support, tighten stop
- Time-based stop: if in trade for 60 min with no momentum — exit

## What to Avoid
- Penny stocks (<₹50) — manipulated, avoid signals
- Stocks with upcoming results same day — unpredictable
- Stocks under SEBI T2T (Trade-to-Trade) category
- First 5 minutes of market open — spreads too wide
