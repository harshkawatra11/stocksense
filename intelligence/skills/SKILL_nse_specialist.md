# SKILL: Indian Market (NSE/BSE) Specialist

## Market Structure
- NSE (National Stock Exchange): Primary exchange, Nifty 50 / Nifty 500 index
- BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange): Sensex 30, larger listed universe
- F&O segment: Futures and Options on ~200 stocks + indices
- Derivatives expiry: Weekly (Thursday) + Monthly (last Thursday)

## Regulatory Framework (SEBI)
- Circuit breakers: 5%, 10%, 20% upper/lower for individual stocks
- T+1 settlement: Trades settle next day
- SEBI Insider Trading Regulations: Watch for unusual volume before results
- Bulk/Block deal disclosures: Available on NSE website — significant signals

## Key Indian Market Calendars
- Budget: February 1 every year — massive sector moves
- RBI Monetary Policy: Every 2 months — impacts banking, NBFC, real estate
- Results season: April-May (Q4), July-Aug (Q1), Oct-Nov (Q2), Jan-Feb (Q3)
- Diwali effect: Pre-Diwali rally common in Oct-Nov
- January effect: FII re-allocation, often strong opening to year
- Expiry week volatility: Every Thursday, especially monthly expiry

## Sector Knowledge

### Banking & NBFC
- NPA (Non-Performing Assets) data = key driver
- RBI rate cuts = positive for all banks, especially PSU banks
- Credit growth data released monthly

### IT Sector
- Revenue in USD — INR depreciation is positive for margins
- US recession fears = negative for Indian IT
- TCS, Infosys, Wipro results set sector tone

### Pharma
- USFDA inspection outcomes = significant stock moves
- Domestic formulations steady; export-dependent stocks more volatile
- Generic pricing pressure from US market

### Metals & Mining
- Commodity prices (iron ore, aluminum, zinc) drive stock prices
- China demand data critical for metals

### Real Estate
- Interest rate sensitive — rate cuts = strong rally
- Affordable housing schemes (PMAY) = midcap boost

## Patterns Specific to Indian Market
- Pre-budget rally: November-January outperformance of infrastructure + defense
- Post-results gap fill: Large gaps often partially fill in 2-3 sessions
- IPO listing effect: Watch for listing day ±5% then drift
- Operator-driven midcaps: Volume + news + no fundamentals = stay away

## Quality Signal Indicators
- High: Large-cap (Nifty 100), high volume, clear fundamental trigger
- Medium: Midcap F&O stocks, pattern-based, no conflicting news
- Low: Small-cap, illiquid, operator-suspected stocks

## Red Flags
- Stock hitting lower circuit on high volume = avoid BUY
- Promoter pledging >50% = governance risk
- Sudden volume 10x with no news = potential manipulation
- Stock name change, merger announcement without clarity = wait
