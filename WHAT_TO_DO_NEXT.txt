================================================================================
STOCKSENSE — WHAT'S ACTUALLY WRONG, AND WHAT TO DO NEXT
Deep audit conducted 2026-07-11 by 6 parallel research agents (models layer,
latency/live-feel, intelligence/docs, backend/scheduler, data/DB/frontend,
plus external broker-API research). Every claim below is backed by file:line
evidence or a sourced external fact — nothing here is guessed.
================================================================================


SECTION 1 — EXECUTIVE VERDICT
================================================================================

StockSense is architecturally ambitious and genuinely well-organized: a clean
4-layer signal engine, a real scheduler running 12 autonomous jobs, an audit
trail, a self-calibrating parameter system, and an 8-tab dashboard. On paper
it reads like a hedge-fund-grade retail tool.

What it actually is today: a SIGNAL VALIDATOR wearing a TRADER'S COSTUME.

Three facts explain why it doesn't stand out yet, and none of them are about
missing features — they're about things silently NOT working while looking
like they are:

  1. Kronos, the "time-series forecaster," is not trained on Indian data.
     It's running the stock pretrained weights from GitHub (Chinese/crypto
     markets), and when even that fails to load, it silently swaps in a
     random-walk mock and keeps emitting "signals" as if nothing happened.

  2. The paper trading account has ₹500 in it. At 10% position sizing that's
     a ₹50 budget per trade — you cannot buy one share of almost any NSE
     stock with that. The auto-trader auto-PASSES on ~99% of signals. The
     "learning loop" has been learning from rejections, not from trades.

  3. The app has no live price feed. Everything is REST-polled on a 30s–30min
     cadence; the "1-minute candles" are fake (flat OHLC snapshots taken
     every 5 minutes). There is no WebSocket anywhere in the stack. This is
     the single biggest reason it feels slow next to Groww/AngelOne — those
     apps tick every second; StockSense refreshes every 30–120 seconds and
     shows you a price that's already 2–7 minutes stale.

The good news: none of this requires a rewrite. The core pipeline (LightGBM +
combine + Claude synthesis), the DB schema, the scheduler, and the frontend
shell are all sound and reusable. What's missing is (a) honesty about what's
actually running, (b) a real-time data layer, and (c) a paper account and
accuracy metric that could plausibly justify real money later. Sections 2-5
lay out exactly what to fix, in what order, and why.


SECTION 2 — THE HARD TRUTHS (full findings, worst first)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  CRITICAL — Kronos is not fine-tuned on NSE data. Ever confirmed your own
     suspicion here: it's true.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evidence:
  - models/kronos/weights/nse_finetuned/nse_all_mini/ contains only empty
    tokenizer/ and logs/ directories. There is NO basemodel/best_model
    checkpoint. This matches your own memory record: the fine-tune job OOM'd
    at step 90.7k/945.9k after 14 hours and Kronos only checkpoints at epoch
    end, so the crash lost everything.
  - models/kronos/integration.py:27-38 globs for a finetuned weights dir,
    finds nothing, and KRONOS_WEIGHTS_DIR resolves to "".
  - models/kronos/integration.py:82-87: when that path doesn't exist, it
    silently loads NeoQuasar/Kronos-mini from HuggingFace — the public
    pretrained checkpoint, trained predominantly on Chinese A-share and
    crypto market data. No log line calls this out as a degraded state;
    it just says "Loading pretrained {model_id}".
  - models/kronos/integration.py:73-94: if even THAT import/load throws
    (GPU OOM, missing deps, CUDA driver issue), the code catches the
    exception and silently returns MockKronosForecaster() — a random-walk
    generator — logged only as a warning. The pipeline has no idea it just
    started hallucinating price paths, and neither does the user.

Why this matters: every "Kronos forecast" ETA and confidence you've seen in
the Intelligence tab is either (a) a Chinese-market model's guess applied to
an Indian stock it's never been trained on, or (b) literal noise. This isn't
a minor accuracy hit — it's a component silently returning garbage while
being weighted 60% in the combine step (see 2.4).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2  HIGH — Kronos's "confidence" score is fabricated, not measured.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - integration.py:154-156: sample_count=1 — a SINGLE sampled path, no
    ensemble, no variance estimate.
  - integration.py:194-204: confidence is a sigmoid of the predicted move's
    magnitude (k=0.45), e.g. predict +0.5% move -> confidence 0.51; predict
    +3% move -> confidence 0.93. This has nothing to do with how sure the
    model actually is — it's confidence-by-optimism. A wilder guess produces
    a higher confidence score, which is backwards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3  CRITICAL — The paper trading account is mathematically insolvent.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - config.py:107 (approx): CASH_AVAILABLE = ₹500.
  - intelligence/auto_trader.py:98-103: budget = cash_available * max_pos_pct
    (default 10%) = ₹50 per trade. qty = int(budget // price). Since almost
    every liquid NSE stock trades ₹200-₹3000+, qty resolves to 0 for the vast
    majority of signals, and auto_trader auto-PASSES with a logged reason
    like "budget ₹50 (10% of ₹500) can't buy 1 share @ ₹291.00".
  - Consequence: the "accuracy" the system reports, and the 55% threshold
    gating PAPER->LIVE mode (trading_mode.py:26), is being computed almost
    entirely on trades that were never executed. The learning loop is
    training on hypotheticals, not outcomes. This one bug alone invalidates
    most of the system's self-reported track record.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4  HIGH — combine.py's model weighting is arbitrary and averages
     incommensurable numbers.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - models/kronos/combine.py:11-14: hardcoded defaults lgbm_weight=0.40,
    kronos_weight=0.60, agreement_boost=0.05 — no comment or citation for
    where these numbers came from.
  - Line 107: combined_conf = ml_conf*0.40 + kr_conf*0.60 + agreement_boost,
    capped at 0.95. This linearly averages a calibrated ML probability with
    a fabricated Kronos sigmoid score (2.2) as if they were the same kind of
    number. They are not.
  - Agreement boost (+0.05) fires whenever ML and Kronos agree on direction
    — but per 2.1, "Kronos" may currently be a random-walk mock, meaning any
    agreement is spurious, not confirmatory.
  - On disagreement, the combined signal collapses to HOLD @ 0.45 confidence,
    discarding whatever signal the stronger model had.
  - DB weight refresh (lines 17-50) silently falls back to the 40/60 default
    if the query fails — no alert.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5  HIGH — The measured trading edge is real but thin, and partly in-sample.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
From research/edge_by_year.csv / .out.txt (your own backtest artifacts):
  - 2026 YTD: net expectancy +25bps/trade, win rate 54.1%.
  - 2025: net expectancy only +4bps/trade, win rate 51.8% — barely above coin
    flip.
  - 2001-2003 shows the strongest expectancy (+50-110bps) but the model was
    trained on the full 2001-2026 history, meaning these years are largely
    IN-SAMPLE, not a genuine out-of-sample track record.
  - 2008 (financial crisis): net expectancy NEGATIVE — the model fails
    exactly when regime shifts happen, which is when you'd most need it to
    work.
  - The strategy is non-selective: it fires 85,000-220,000 signals per year
    across ~2,000-3,700 tickers. That's closer to "score everything" than
    "find the best trades."
  - Round-trip transaction costs (~25-35bps: brokerage + STT + slippage) eat
    most or all of the edge in an average year. 2025's +4bps is a loss after
    costs.

Verdict: there is a small, real, statistically-detectable directional edge in
the LightGBM layer. It is not currently large enough, nor validated cleanly
enough out-of-sample, to justify real capital. This is fixable (Phase 2) but
must be said plainly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6  HIGH — "Learnings" accumulate forever with no expiry or validation.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - intelligence/eod_review.py:74-104: learnings are deduplicated only by a
    title+body hash over the trailing 90 days. There is no expiry, no
    per-learning accuracy tracking, and no mechanism to demote or delete a
    learning that turns out to be wrong.
  - A learning written in a bull regime ("post-rally breakouts continue") is
    fed verbatim into Claude's synthesis prompt indefinitely — including
    during a bear regime where it's actively wrong.
  - This makes the "compounding learning loop" marketed in the README
    unfalsifiable: nothing is ever proven false and removed, so bad patterns
    accumulate alongside good ones with no way to tell them apart.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.7  HIGH — Nightly parameter calibration is noise-chasing.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - intelligence/calibration.py:76-78: gathers ONLY the current day's signal
    outcomes (typically 30-40 signals) and asks Claude to propose bounded
    brain_params changes based on that single day.
  - One day of ~30-40 outcomes is statistical noise, not a trend. Running
    this nightly means confidence_threshold and other knobs can ping-pong
    up and down based on which way a handful of trades happened to go,
    with no smoothing and no rollback if the next day's accuracy gets worse.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.8  HIGH — No live price feed anywhere; app runs on 30s-30min stale REST
     polling. This is the #1 reason it doesn't feel alive.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full latency chain (worst case ~7 minutes, typical 2-3 minutes):
  - "Live" prices come from Groww's 5-minute intraday snapshot job
    (scheduler/market_runner.py:182-188 -> data/pipeline/fetch_groww.py:
    265-276), which writes a single LTP value into ohlcv_1min as a FAKE
    1-minute candle: flat OHLC (open=high=low=close=LTP), zero volume. It
    looks like granular data; it is a snapshot taken once every 5 minutes.
  - The frontend polls indices every 30s-2min (useMarketIndices.ts:11-12)
    and the entire Live tab (6 separate endpoints: signals, account,
    activity, position reviews, status, mode) every 60 seconds
    (Live.tsx:89), doing a full re-fetch and full re-render each time —
    no incremental/delta updates.
  - Signal cards display price_at_signal, frozen at the moment the signal
    fired. There is NO live current price shown anywhere on a signal card
    (Live.tsx:165-229). You cannot currently watch a position's live P&L
    move.
  - An SSE stream already exists (/api/stream/signals, backend/main.py:
    63-147) but it only carries pipeline STAGE events (ML done -> Kronos
    done -> SLM done), never price ticks. It's plumbing built for the wrong
    thing.
  - Angel One's index feed has a circuit breaker that, after one failure,
    hides index prices for 5 minutes with zero user-facing status — indices
    just silently vanish or freeze.
  - The 30-minute signal generation cadence, plus a deliberate 10-minute
    stagger between signal_pipeline and position_review jobs (to avoid GPU
    contention on the 4GB card, market_runner.py:327-339), means the
    average signal a user sees is already ~15 minutes old by the time it's
    acted on.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.9  MEDIUM — Silent failure is a pattern across the whole intelligence
     layer, not a one-off bug.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - intelligence/llm_cli.py:151-179: if the Claude/Codex/Gemini CLI binary
    isn't found, or times out, or errors after retries, the function returns
    "" (empty string). Every caller checks `if out:` and just skips synthesis
    silently. The dashboard has no indicator that "Claude synthesis" — the
    marketed final validation layer — didn't run on a given cycle.
  - intelligence/macro_context.py: if Ollama/qwen2.5 times out (300s) or
    errors, returns a neutral context (all 14 sectors = 0.0). The confidence
    nudge (+-0.10) then applies zero adjustment, indistinguishable from "the
    market genuinely has no sector bias today."
  - models/ml/predict.py:36-46: model hot-reload check wraps a file mtime
    read in `except OSError: pass` — if it fails, you silently keep serving
    a stale model with no warning.
  - Net effect: at any given moment, an unknown subset of "AI-verified"
    signals never actually got AI-verified, and there is currently no way
    to tell which ones from the UI.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.10 MEDIUM — Accuracy is not a well-defined metric, and costs aren't
     modeled anywhere.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - accuracy_tracker.py:14-44 counts a signal "correct" on status='hit_target'
    but the handling of hit_stop vs. expired-with-no-resolution is unclear
    and inconsistently penalized.
  - No transaction cost (brokerage/STT/slippage, ~25-35bps round trip) is
    ever subtracted from any reported accuracy or expectancy number outside
    the one-off research/edge_by_year.py script. The live accuracy_tracker
    used to gate PAPER->LIVE mode does not apply costs.
  - There's no benchmark: nothing compares the strategy's returns to simply
    buying and holding NIFTYBEES (~12-14%/yr with zero skill required).
    Without that comparison, "55% win rate" has no context for whether the
    system is actually adding value.
  - No portfolio-level risk limits exist: no cap on concentration in one
    stock/sector, no daily loss circuit breaker, no max drawdown guard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.11 MEDIUM — Stops are only checked at end-of-day, not intraday.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Position stop-loss / target logic lives in eod_review.py, evaluated once
    at 15:45 IST. A stop breached at 10:30 AM sits unexited for 5+ hours,
    meaning realized losses can be materially worse than the strategy's own
    stated stop levels.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.12 LightGBM layer itself: methodology is actually fine, mostly.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the one component that holds up reasonably well under scrutiny:
labels are correctly forward-shifted (no lookahead bias,
feature_engineering.py:254-255), TimeSeriesSplit walk-forward CV is used,
class imbalance is handled via scale_pos_weight, and the decision threshold
is F1-optimized rather than a naive 0.5. Two real issues remain:
  - The deployed model (lgbm_latest.pkl) is 37 days stale as of this audit
    (last trained June 4), with no visible auto-retrain having fired despite
    retrain_trigger.py existing for exactly this purpose.
  - F&O features (OI, PCR, delivery %) silently default to 0/1.0 when F&O
    data isn't available for a ticker (feature_engineering.py:239-251) —
    since F&O data only exists from 2019 onward but training data goes back
    to 2001, there's a silent train/serve feature distribution mismatch for
    a meaningful chunk of training history.


SECTION 3 — BROKER API DECISION: SWITCH THE DATA LAYER TO UPSTOX
================================================================================

You raised three points — network IP changes on every laptop boot, Angel
One's twice-weekly IP-change limit, and Groww being paid. Here's the
researched, verified picture as of July 2026:

  - This is NOT an Angel One quirk. SEBI's algo-trading circular, effective
    April 1 2026, requires ORDER traffic (not data/quote traffic) at ALL
    Indian brokers to originate from a registered static IP, changeable at
    most once per calendar week, with one primary + one optional secondary
    IP allowed. Angel One, Upstox, Zerodha, Dhan, Fyers — all of them.

  - The critical distinction: at Upstox specifically, this restriction
    applies ONLY to the Order API. The Market Data Feed (WebSocket V3, LTPC/
    quote/full modes, up to 5,000 instrument keys on the LTPC tier) and the
    historical/intraday candle REST endpoints are explicitly NOT subject to
    static-IP whitelisting. You can stream live quotes from a residential
    connection with a dynamic IP with zero friction.

  - Since StockSense is currently 100% paper trading (no live order
    placement exists anywhere in the codebase), the static-IP problem is
    IRRELEVANT to you right now. It only becomes relevant when/if you flip
    on live order execution later (see Phase 2/3 gate) — and even then,
    it's solvable with a secondary IP, a cheap static-IP add-on from your
    ISP, or a small always-on VPS as a proxy, once per week is a non-issue.

  - Cost comparison (verified):
      Upstox: data APIs free (both quotes and historical), WebSocket feed
              free, no IP restriction on data. Historical 1-min candles
              limited to ~1 month lookback (less deep than Zerodha, fine
              since NSE Bhavcopy already covers EOD history).
      Groww:  ₹499 + GST/month flat, covers all APIs including live data —
              you currently have NO subscription, so this is dead/unused
              code today (see memory: "Groww = live feed pending user's API
              subscription + .env creds").
      Zerodha Kite Connect: ₹500/month, deepest historical 1-min data of
              the three, but no cost advantage over Upstox for your use case.

  - RECOMMENDATION: Migrate the live/intraday data layer to Upstox
    (free, no IP restriction, WebSocket-native). Keep NSE Bhavcopy as the
    authoritative EOD historical source (it already is). Retire the unused
    Groww code path. Keep Angel One wired up only as a parked fallback
    provider behind the existing intelligence/provider_config.py
    abstraction — don't delete it, just stop depending on it as primary.


SECTION 4 — THE ROADMAP
================================================================================
Total estimate: ~21-29 solo developer-days. Sequencing matters — Phase 0
must come first because it's the foundation everything else is measured
against; you cannot trust Phase 2's backtest numbers, or Phase 4's "live
P&L" ticker, if the system underneath is still silently lying about what
ran.

Sequencing: Phase 0 -> Phase 1 -> (Phase 2 and Phase 4 can run in parallel,
but Phase 2 item 3 [intraday stops] needs Phase 1's price feed) -> Phase 3.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 0 — TRUTH LAYER  (3-4 days, do this first, no dependencies)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: the app must never silently pretend a degraded component is healthy.

Tasks:
  1. models/kronos/integration.py: replace the silent pretrained-fallback
     (lines 27-38, 82-87) and the MockKronosForecaster catch-all (73-94)
     with an explicit model_source field: "finetuned" | "pretrained" | "mock"
     | "unavailable". When source is "mock", exclude Kronos from combine.py
     entirely rather than blending in random-walk noise.
  2. Apply the same explicit-status pattern to:
       - models/ml/predict.py:36-46 (hot-reload swallow)
       - intelligence/llm_cli.py:151-179 (empty-string treated as success)
       - intelligence/macro_context.py (silent neutral fallback)
  3. New backend/routers/system_health.py exposing GET /api/system/health:
     per-component status (LightGBM model age, Kronos source, macro LLM
     reachability, Claude CLI reachability, data freshness via existing
     intelligence/data_freshness.py, Angel circuit-breaker state).
  4. Persist the component-status snapshot alongside each signal (new
     components_json column) so history is auditable after the fact —
     you should be able to ask "was Kronos real on the day this signal
     fired?"
  5. Redefine accuracy honestly in intelligence/accuracy_tracker.py: outcome
     = realized return NET of a ~0.30% round-trip cost constant. Replace the
     "55% win rate" PAPER->LIVE gate (trading_mode.py:26) with something
     like "net expectancy > 0 over the trailing 60 EXECUTED (not just
     suggested) trades."
  6. Frontend: a persistent SystemHealthBar component, plus inline badges on
     signal cards, e.g. "Kronos: pretrained (not NSE-tuned)" or "Claude:
     skipped this cycle."

Definition of done: delete the Kronos weights directory and restart the
pipeline — within one cycle, the UI visibly says Kronos is unavailable, and
Kronos's weight in combine.py drops to zero rather than defaulting to noise.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 — LIVE DATA LAYER VIA UPSTOX  (5-7 days, depends on Phase 0's
health surfacing existing so the migration's failures are visible)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: sub-2-second price staleness, real ticking, honest candles.

Architecture: Upstox WebSocket V3 (LTPC feed) -> in-memory quote cache ->
backend WebSocket fan-out -> frontend live hook. Signal generation stays on
its existing 30-minute cadence (it's compute-heavy by nature) but price
DISPLAY becomes fully decoupled and near-instant.

Tasks:
  1. New data/pipeline/upstox_client.py: auth (access token via env, manual
     daily refresh is fine to start), NSE_EQ instrument-key resolution,
     REST historical/intraday candle fetchers.
  2. New data/pipeline/upstox_feed.py: WebSocket V3 protobuf LTPC consumer
     with reconnect/backoff, subscribing to index symbols + all currently
     "actionable" signal tickers + watchlist tickers.
  3. New backend/services/quote_cache.py: an in-memory {symbol: (ltp,
     prev_close, timestamp)} cache, populated by a FastAPI lifespan
     background task, with per-symbol staleness computed on read.
  4. New backend/routers/ws_prices.py: WS /api/ws/prices — sends a full
     snapshot on connect, then throttled (~1s) delta ticks thereafter.
     Leave the existing SSE stream (main.py:63-147) as-is; it's the right
     tool for pipeline-stage events, just not for prices.
  5. New frontend/src/hooks/useRealtimePrices.ts (WebSocket client with
     reconnect + staleness flag), wired into Live.tsx's signal cards to
     show live LTP and live move-vs-price_at_signal, and into
     useMarketIndices.ts to replace REST polling.
  6. Delete the fake-candle Groww snapshot path (fetch_groww.py:265-276,
     scheduler/market_runner.py:182-188). Intraday features derive from
     real Upstox 1-min OHLCV instead. NSE Bhavcopy remains the EOD
     source of truth; add a nightly Upstox-close vs. Bhavcopy-close
     reconciliation check to market_runner.py that alerts on >0.1%
     mismatch (catches feed drift early).
  7. Move Angel One's index-quote path behind intelligence/provider_config.py
     as an explicit fallback provider, not the primary.

Definition of done: during market hours, prices on screen tick within ~1
second of what the actual Upstox app shows for the same symbol. Killing your
wifi shows a "stale" badge within ~5 seconds and the feed auto-recovers on
reconnect without a restart.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 — RIGOR LAYER  (7-10 days, needs Phase 0's cost constant; item 3
below needs Phase 1's quote cache)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: a paper track record that could plausibly justify real capital someday.

Tasks:
  1. Refund the paper account: config.py CASH_AVAILABLE -> ₹100,000 (gives
     ~₹10,000/position at 10% sizing, enough to actually buy real quantities
     of most NSE stocks). Mark this as "epoch 2" in trading_account.py's
     ledger so the insolvent ₹500-era history is excluded from reported
     stats rather than silently blended in.
  2. New intelligence/costs.py: one shared cost model (STT on sell,
     brokerage, a slippage estimate) consumed identically by auto_trader,
     accuracy_tracker, and the new backtester below — so "accuracy" always
     means the same thing everywhere.
  3. Intraday stop execution: intelligence/position_monitor.py polls the
     Phase 1 quote_cache every 5-10 seconds and exits immediately on a stop
     breach, instead of waiting for the 15:45 EOD review.
  4. New backtest/walkforward.py + backtest/report.py: a full-pipeline
     walk-forward harness (expanding-window retrain, not the current
     single 80/20 split) that replays feature engineering -> LightGBM ->
     combine (Kronos/macro either replayed from stored historical outputs
     or excluded, pending the Phase 3 decision below) and reports a
     net-of-cost equity curve, expectancy, max drawdown, and turnover
     against a NIFTYBEES buy-and-hold benchmark. Exclude 2001-2003 from
     headline numbers since that period is in-sample (see 2.5).
  5. Learnings lifecycle: extend eod_review.py's learnings table with
     created_at, applies_count, and a running hit-rate; auto-expire a
     learning after 60 days or if its hit-rate drops below 50% over at
     least 10 applications; cap active learnings at 20 so the synthesis
     prompt doesn't bloat indefinitely.
  6. Move calibration from nightly to weekly: relocate calibration.py's
     trigger from market_runner.py to scheduler/weekend_job.py, require a
     minimum of 15 outcomes before adjusting anything, apply an EWMA
     smoother instead of raw daily deltas, and add a rollback if the
     following week's accuracy gets worse.
  7. Extend intelligence/portfolio_guard.py with real risk limits: max 8
     concurrent positions, max 25% capital in any one sector, and a 2%
     daily-loss circuit breaker that halts new auto-trades for the rest
     of the day.

Definition of done: `python -m backtest.walkforward --from 2019` produces a
net-of-cost report showing the strategy's equity curve against NIFTYBEES;
paper trades now fill with non-zero, realistic quantities; a stop breach
during market hours triggers an exit within seconds, not hours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3 — THE KRONOS DECISION  (2-3 days, do this after Phase 2's harness
exists so any resurrection attempt can be objectively measured)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDATION: Drop Kronos. Replace it with a 3-seed LightGBM ensemble plus
quantile regressors (q10/q50/q90 of forward 5-day return).

Reasoning: Kronos has never once run as originally designed in this project
— the fine-tune never completed, and what's live today is either an
off-domain pretrained checkpoint or a random-walk mock (Section 2.1). Its
confidence score is fabricated by construction (2.2), and it monopolizes the
only 4GB GPU in the system, which is the direct cause of the 10-minute job
stagger that slows the whole pipeline down (2.8). Meanwhile, the only
measured, real, positive edge in the entire system lives in the LightGBM
layer (2.5, 2.12). Finishing the NSE fine-tune properly (mid-epoch
checkpointing, fp16, smaller micro-batches, or renting a cloud GPU for a
weekend) is real effort spent chasing a component that has never been
validated to add value even in principle, on top of a domain shift that's
non-trivial to fix (Indian equities behave very differently from Chinese
A-shares and crypto).

This doesn't have to be permanent. If you want to keep the door open: build
the ensemble first, get it into the Phase 2 walk-forward harness, and only
resurrect a properly fine-tuned Kronos later if it can be shown to beat the
ensemble in that same harness. Don't let "the impressive foundation model"
win by default over "the thing that's actually working."

Tasks: add a 3-seed LightGBM ensemble + quantile regressors to
models/ml/train.py (confidence = ensemble agreement, target/stop derived
from the quantiles instead of a Kronos path); simplify combine.py to
LightGBM + macro nudge + Claude synthesis, with weights derived from the
walk-forward harness's IC rather than the current unjustified 40/60 + 0.05;
remove the GPU stagger from market_runner.py:327-339 now that only one
model needs the GPU (LightGBM training is cheap/CPU-friendly); archive
models/kronos/ behind a feature flag rather than deleting it outright.

Definition of done: the pipeline runs end-to-end with no Kronos import; the
ensemble's walk-forward net expectancy is >= the old combined model's; total
pipeline wall-clock time per cycle drops.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4 — MAKE IT FEEL ALIVE  (4-5 days, depends on Phase 1's price feed;
can run in parallel with Phase 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the direct answer to "how do I make it feel like Groww/AngelOne but
smarter." Priority order, biggest perceived impact first:

  1. Ticking prices with a green/red flash on change, on every price shown
     anywhere (signal cards, indices, open positions). This single change
     is the biggest "it's alive" cue a trading UI can have.
  2. Live unrealized P&L on open positions, recomputed on every tick by
     joining the quote_cache into backend/routers/portfolio.py and
     usePortfolio.ts — watching a number move in real time is what makes a
     terminal feel like a terminal instead of a report.
  3. A price-age indicator dot on every quote: green under 2 seconds, amber
     under 30 seconds, red/stale beyond that. When the market's closed,
     honestly label everything "as of 15:30 close" instead of implying
     freshness it doesn't have.
  4. A genuinely streaming activity feed: extend the existing SSE stream
     (main.py:63-147) to also emit fills, stop triggers, new learnings, and

SECTION 5 — WHAT NOT TO DO
================================================================================

  1. Don't chase live order execution yet. The paper account isn't even
     honest yet (Section 2.3), the accuracy metric isn't cost-adjusted
     (2.10), and there's no walk-forward-validated edge (2.5) to trade with
     real money. Live execution is a Phase 5+ conversation, gated on Phase
     2's rigor work actually shipping — not on the SEBI static-IP rule,
     which (Section 3) isn't your blocker right now.
  2. Don't resume the Kronos fine-tune on the 4GB GPU as-is. It already
     OOM'd once and lost 14 hours because Kronos only checkpoints at epoch
     end. If you ever do revisit fine-tuning (post-Phase-3, only if the
     ensemble loses to it), fix mid-epoch checkpointing and reduce batch
     size first, or just rent a cloud GPU for a weekend — don't repeat the
     same failure mode a second time.
  3. Don't add new dashboard tabs, new signal types, or new "AI features"
     before Phase 0 ships. Every new feature built on top of a system that
     silently fails without telling anyone just adds more surface area that
     can be silently broken. Truth first, then speed, then rigor, then
     polish — in that order, because each phase's numbers depend on the
     one before it being trustworthy.
  4. Don't keep the Groww integration around as "maybe useful later" dead
     weight once Upstox is live — it's an unpaid, unused code path today;
     either subscribe and use it as a genuine secondary provider, or retire
     it. Don't leave ambiguous half-wired providers in provider_config.py.
  5. Don't trust any accuracy number quoted anywhere in the app (dashboard,
     logs, or this document's Section 2.5) as investment-grade until Phase
     2's cost-adjusted, benchmark-compared, walk-forward harness has run.
     Everything before that is directionally informative, not decision-grade.
