# SKILL: Indian Macro Cycle — NSE Impact Playbook

## Overview
Indian macroeconomic cycles create predictable sector rotation patterns, valuation re-ratings, and FII flow dynamics on NSE. This playbook maps key macro indicators — RBI policy, Union Budget, election cycles, global commodity prices, monsoon, and US Fed decisions — to specific NSE sector and stock behavior.

---

## 1. RBI Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) — Calendar and Market Impact

### MPC Meeting Schedule
- **6 meetings per year**: Approximately February, April, June, August, October, December
- Policy announcement: Day 3 of the 3-day meeting (typically Wednesday or Friday at 10 AM IST)
- Minutes released: 2 weeks after the meeting
- Key rates: Repo Rate (primary), Reverse Repo Rate (liquidity), CRR (cash reserve ratio), SLR

### Rate Decision → Sector Impact Matrix

#### Rate Hike Scenario (Hawkish)
| Sector | Short-Term (1–2 weeks) | Medium-Term (1–3 months) |
|--------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Private Banks | SELL — NIM pressure, rising cost of funds | Neutral if rate hike cycle near end |
| PSU Banks | SELL — higher NPAs feared, govt bond losses | May recover if economy stays strong |
| NBFCs | SELL — borrowing costs rise, AUM growth slows | Avoid until rate peak signals |
| Housing Finance (HFC) | SELL — home loan rates rise, demand slowdown | 6-month lag impact on disbursements |
| IT (large-cap) | BUY — USD strengthens vs INR; US growth narrative | Outperform in hike cycles |
| Auto (4-wheeler) | SELL — EMI burden rises, discretionary slowdown | 3-month lag before volume impact |
| FMCG | Neutral to mild SELL — defensive but rate sensitive at high PE | Hold quality names |
| Real Estate | SELL — most rate-sensitive sector on NSE | Avoid until rate plateau confirmed |
| Capital Goods | Mild SELL — project financing costs rise | Infrastructure order book still intact |

#### Rate Cut Scenario (Dovish)
| Sector | Short-Term (1–2 weeks) | Medium-Term (1–3 months) |
|--------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Real Estate | STRONG BUY — home loan affordability improves | Rerate significantly |
| NBFC | BUY — borrowing costs fall, spread widens | Phoenix play after rate-hike damage |
| Auto | BUY — EMI drops, 2W rural demand surges first | 2-wheeler + tractor before 4-wheeler |
| Banks (Private) | BUY but cautious — NIM compression risk in cut cycle | Selective: low-cost-deposit banks win |
| Infrastructure/Roads | BUY — government capex costs fall | Multi-month play |
| IT | SELL signal — rate cuts signal US slowdown fears | Check US demand guidance simultaneously |
| Gold ETFs | BUY — real yield falls globally | Gold outperforms in rate-cut cycles |

### Tone Categories and Trading Response
- **Hawkish surprise** (hike > expected or no cut when cut was priced): Bank Nifty -1.5% to -3% intraday, Nifty -0.8% to -1.5%
- **Dovish surprise** (cut > expected or pause when hike was priced): Bank Nifty +2% to +4% intraday
- **In-line decision**: Reaction to governor commentary tone; watch for "withdrawal of accommodation" vs "accommodative stance"

## 2. Union Budget Seasonality

### Pre-Budget Rally (November–January)
- Historically, Nifty rallies 8–15% in the 3 months before the February 1 Budget
- Key sectors pumped pre-budget by expectation speculation:
  - **Infrastructure, Capital Goods, Defense**: Government spending hopes
  - **FMCG, Rural Consumption**: Income tax relief and rural spend expectations
  - **Railways, PSUs**: Capex allocation anticipation
- Entry strategy: Build positions in expected beneficiary sectors by mid-November; exit by January 31 (pre-event exit)

### Budget Day (February 1) — "Sell the News"
- Market tends to SELL on Budget day even with positive announcements if rally was already large
- Sectors explicitly named in budget for capex/PLI allocation: Initial spike, then profit-taking within 3–5 days
- Tax changes on capital gains (LTCG/STCG): Immediate negative if tax increased; positive if rationalized
- 2024 LTCG increase from 10% to 12.5% caused 2% intraday Nifty drop on Budget day

### Post-Budget Playbook
- **Week 1 post-Budget**: Sort through announcements; find under-hyped beneficiaries (smaller caps in beneficiary sectors)
- **February–March**: Tax-saving investment season (ELSS flows into mutual funds = buying pressure on MF-heavy stocks)
- **March 31**: Year-end window dressing by mutual funds — MF portfolio stocks get last-week buying

## 3. Election Cycle Impact

### Pre-Election Phase (12 Months Before General Election)
- Government maximizes visible spending in:
  - **Infrastructure, Roads, Railways**: Order book expansion
  - **PSU Banks**: Credit growth push, NPA forbearance
  - **Defense**: Indigenization contracts, Make in India
  - **Agriculture**: Loan waivers, MSP hikes → FMCG rural, fertilizers
  - **Housing for All schemes**: Cement, Steel, Building materials
- NSE pattern: PSU basket outperforms private sector by 15–25% in pre-election year

### Election Result Day (Counting Day)
- **Ruling party wins big**: Nifty +3% to +7% gap-up; PSUs, Infra, Defense surge
- **Hung parliament / coalition**: Nifty -5% to -10%; extreme volatility; avoid holding overnight into counting
- **Opposition win**: Uncertain; markets reprice policy continuity risk; PSUs sell off

### Post-Election (6–12 Months After)
- Profit-taking on pre-election infra/PSU rally: Trim by 30–50% in month 1 post-results
- New government's first budget (if applicable) sets multi-year sector priorities
- Historical pattern: Nifty typically flat to -5% in 3 months after election result (regardless of outcome)

### State Elections
- Affects: FMCG (rural consumption schemes), PSU Banks (agri loan waiver fears), Alcohol stocks (state excise policy)
- Less market-wide impact; more sector/state-specific

## 4. FII Flow Patterns

### USD/DXY Relationship
- **DXY rises (USD strengthens)**: FIIs sell EM including India to repatriate USD gains
  - INR depreciates, Nifty typically -1.5% to -4% over 2 weeks of sustained DXY rise
  - Worst affected: BFSI, Consumer, Domestic cyclicals
  - Least affected (natural hedge): IT exporters (benefit from INR depreciation)
- **DXY falls (USD weakens)**: FII inflows into EM; India typically receives $500M–$2B in weeks
  - Nifty +2% to +5%; Banking and quality growth stocks outperform

### INR Stability as FII Anchor
- **USD/INR < 83**: FII comfortable holding India positions; equity flows positive
- **USD/INR 83–85**: Caution zone; FII reduce marginal additions
- **USD/INR > 85**: Accelerated FII selling; "carry unwind" risk
- RBI intervenes via USD sales to defend INR — watch RBI forex reserves depletion as indicator of stress duration

### Seasonal FII Flow Patterns
- **January–February**: Global rebalancing; FII often sell to take profits from prior year
- **April–June**: Post-results FII reassessment; flows depend on earnings season quality
- **September–November**: Year-end window by global funds; typically FII buying if India earnings strong
- **December**: Low volume; FII holiday season; thin markets amplify moves

## 5. GST Collection as Consumption Proxy

### Data Release
- Released on **1st of every month** for previous month's collection
- Below ₹1.5 lakh crore = weak consumption; Above ₹1.7 lakh crore = strong

### GST Collection → Stock Correlation
| GST Level (Monthly) | Signal | Beneficiary Stocks |
|---------------------|--------|-------------------|
| > ₹1.8 lakh crore | Boom | FMCG, Auto, Consumer durables, Retail |
| ₹1.5–1.8 lakh crore | Healthy growth | Broad market positive |
| ₹1.2–1.5 lakh crore | Slowing | Defensive positioning |
| < ₹1.2 lakh crore | Stress | Avoid consumption stocks |

### Festival Season Check (Oct–Nov GST)
- Festive GST collections: Key indicator for auto and FMCG Q3 volume guidance
- If October GST > ₹1.7 lakh crore: Overweight Maruti, Tata Motors, HUL, Titan for Q3 earnings plays

## 6. PMI as Leading Indicator

### Manufacturing PMI (Released: 1st Business Day of Month)
- **> 55**: Strong expansion — overweight Capital Goods, Auto components, Steel, Cement
- **50–55**: Moderate growth — maintain positions
- **47–50**: Near contraction — reduce cyclicals
- **< 47**: Contraction — avoid industrials; shift to defensives

### Services PMI (Released: 3rd Business Day of Month)
- **> 58**: Boom — buy BFSI, IT services, Consumer discretionary
- **54–58**: Healthy — maintain
- **50–54**: Slowing — cautious on discretionary
- **< 50**: Contraction — rare in India; indicates systemic stress; defensive mode

### PMI vs. Nifty Correlation
- Manufacturing PMI leading Nifty by ~2 months (historically 0.6–0.7 correlation)
- Use: PMI decline for 2 consecutive months = advance warning for Nifty correction
- PMI recovery for 2 consecutive months = recovery confirmation; start adding cyclicals

## 7. Monsoon Impact

### Monsoon Calendar
- **June 1**: Kerala landfall (official monsoon onset)
- **July 15**: Maximum coverage target across India
- **September 30**: Withdrawal begins
- IMD (India Meteorological Department) gives seasonal forecast in April (watch carefully)

### Monsoon → Stock Sector Impact
| Monsoon Type | FMCG Rural | Agri/Fertilizer | 2W Auto | Microfinance (MFI) | Cement |
|-------------|-----------|----------------|---------|-------------------|--------|
| Normal/Above normal | BUY | BUY | STRONG BUY | BUY | BUY |
| Slightly below (-5%) | Hold | Neutral | Neutral | Caution | Neutral |
| Deficient (< 90% of LPA) | SELL | SELL | SELL | SELL rural MFI | Mild sell |
| Drought | Major SELL | SELL | SELL | Immediate SELL | SELL |

### Specific NSE Stocks to Track
- **Good monsoon beneficiaries**: HUL, Dabur, Marico (rural FMCG), Hero MotoCorp, Bajaj Auto (2W), UPL, PI Industries (agrochemicals), Tractor companies (M&M, Escorts)
- **Bad monsoon hedges**: Urban consumption stocks (Titan, Westside, Avenue Supermarts)

### Weekly Monsoon Data
- IMD releases weekly actual vs. normal monsoon data; cumulative deficit/surplus
- Cumulative deficit > 10% by July 31: Sell rural consumption immediately
- Cumulative surplus > 10% by July 31: Add rural-facing names with conviction

## 8. Crude Oil Price Impact

### The ₹10 / $10 Rule
- **Every $10 rise in Brent crude = ~0.3–0.4% Nifty headwind** (3-month lagged impact on earnings)
- Immediate impact sectors vs. lagged impact:

| Sector | Direct Impact | Magnitude (per $10 crude rise) |
|--------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Aviation (IndiGo, SpiceJet) | IMMEDIATE SELL | −8% to −15% stock |
| Paints (Asian Paints, Berger) | 1–2 month lag | −5% to −8% stock |
| Tyres (MRF, CEAT, Apollo) | 1–2 month lag | −4% to −7% stock |
| OMC (HPCL, BPCL, IOC) | SELL if govt doesn't allow price hike | −10% to −20% |
| Petrochemicals | Immediate raw material cost impact | −5% to −10% |
| Chemicals (specialty) | Moderate; depends on pricing power | −2% to −5% |

### Crude Rise Beneficiaries (Long Side)
- **ONGC, Oil India**: Higher upstream realization — BUY on every $5+ crude rise
- **Shipping (SCI)**: Freight rates correlated with energy demand — BUY
- **Gas utilities (GAIL, Indraprastha Gas)**: Mixed — depends on domestic gas price policy

### Crude Fall Beneficiaries
- Aviation: Every $10 crude drop = ~8–10% EPS improvement for IndiGo
- Paints, Tyres: Margin expansion lag 1–2 quarters
- Consumer goods: Lower input costs, but competitive pricing pressure limits margin gain

## 9. US Federal Reserve Decisions and Nifty Reaction

### Historical Nifty Response to Fed Actions
| Fed Action | Nifty Reaction (1 Week) | FII Flow Pattern |
|-----------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Rate Hike (expected) | −0.5% to −1.5% | Mild FII selling |
| Rate Hike (hawkish surprise) | −2% to −4% | Heavy FII outflow ($1–3B) |
| Rate Pause (first after hike cycle) | +1% to +3% | FII inflow begins |
| Rate Cut (first cut signal) | +2% to +4% | Strong FII inflow ($2–5B) |
| Rate Cut + Dovish guidance | +3% to +5% | Large EM rally; India outperforms |
| Quantitative Tightening (QT) | −1% to −2% delayed | Gradual FII outflow |

### FOMC Meeting Calendar Trade Strategy
- **2 weeks before FOMC**: Reduce open positions by 20%; increase cash buffer
- **FOMC day (India time: 11:30 PM or 2 AM IST)**: Check GIFT Nifty response immediately
- **Next morning**: Trade the confirmed direction — do not fade Fed-driven moves
- **Fed Pivot signal** (first dovish language after hike cycle): One of highest-conviction BUY signals for Indian equities

### USD/INR as Real-Time Fed Sentiment Gauge
- USD/INR drops on dovish Fed: Immediate buy signal for Indian banks and consumer stocks
- USD/INR spikes on hawkish Fed: Sell signal for same-day positions; reduce heat

## 10. China PMI and Indian Metals Sector

### China Manufacturing PMI Release
- Released on last day of every month (official NBS PMI) and first day of next month (Caixin PMI)
- **China PMI > 52**: Metal stocks BUY — steel, aluminum, zinc demand expectations rise
- **China PMI 50–52**: Neutral; hold existing metals positions
- **China PMI < 50**: SELL metals — China is world's largest consumer of all base metals

### China PMI → NSE Metals Sector Correlation
| China PMI Change | NSE Metal Index Expected Move | Key Stocks |
|-----------------|------------------------------|-----------|
| +1 point above 52 | +3% to +5% | Tata Steel, JSW Steel, Hindalco, NALCO |
| Surprise drop below 50 | −5% to −10% | Sell all metal longs immediately |
| Infrastructure stimulus announcement | +8% to +15% | Tata Steel, JSPL, SAIL |

### China-India Metals Decoupling Cases
- When India's own infrastructure capex is high (Budget-driven): Indian steel companies decouple partially from China PMI
- Aluminum: Highly correlated with China PMI (China produces 57% of global aluminum)
- Specialty metals (Graphite India for graphite electrodes): More correlated with global EAF steelmaking than China PMI
