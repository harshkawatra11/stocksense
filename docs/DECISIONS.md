## Phase 3

- T3.1 (2026-11-11): nodes are symbols (same symbol = same sid, so a symbol never maps to two sids and CA/symbol joins can use the symbol alone; `valid_from/valid_to` are informational). Edge B additionally skips a candidate B that already has an incoming official edge (a node may have one in-edge). Official edges are accepted closest-date-first, auto edges by `(last_date, symbol)`; a union-find rejects cycles. "Sessions between" is the difference of 0-based indices in the distinct `bhavcopy_eq` date list (all series). `sid_merges.csv` is appended to across runs; `rename_autodetect.csv` is rewritten each run. If an earlier auto-detected edge disappears later (a second candidate shows up), the two symbols keep their (shared) existing sid; sids are never re-numbered.
- T3.2 (2026-11-11): `compute_gap_factors(rows, ca)` takes `rows` = (sid, date, session_idx, open, close, prev_close) and `ca` with `sid` already joined (an optional `face_fallback` column feeds rights), and returns a SPARSE audit frame (only rows needing a factor, plus `ca_ignored` rows dated at the ex-date; extra columns `ca_types`, `note`); `build_daily_panel` joins it to the full rows (gap_factor 1.0, gap_src '' elsewhere). Every delivery or intraday BUY also leaves a cushion of (DP + minimum brokerage) x (1 + GST) = Rs 29.5 of cash, and a SELL whose costs would overdraw cash is rejected `cost_exceeds_cash`: without these a dust position (value below its own exit fees) drove cash negative, breaking the cash >= -0.01 invariant. An intraday position that cannot trade at the close (locked, no bar) is closed by force at the last close with fees and no slippage, reason `squareoff_forced`. Delisting write-off follows the card (20 sessions without a row and none later in the data -> 80% of the last close, no costs). Reference policies: BuyAndHold, EqualWeightUniverse (open of t+1; trades only names more than `band` off target), and the sleeve policies Clairvoyant / RandomPolicy (one sleeve of 1/h of equity per session: buy at the close of t+1, sell at the open of t+1+h). `labels/forward.py` does not exist on this branch, so `c2o_forward_labels(daily, sessions, h)` in engine.py computes the clairvoyant label from adj_close / adj_open. Truth test (synthetic market, planted_ic=0.10): Clairvoyant (k=5, h=5, Rs 25 lakh) net Sharpe 16.7; RandomPolicy (k=10) mean daily net return -17.5 bps against an expectation of -16.9 bps (1 SE 3.2 bps; the expectation is mean(sleeve fraction) x mean label minus the measured fee+slippage drag). Rs 5 lakh was too small for that test: with k=20 the Rs 5,000 positions pay about 2% round-trip in fixed fees and the book decays to nothing, which is the fixed-fee effect the card wants to see, not a leak. Engine runtime: 0.15 s for the 601-session synthetic market (40 names); 0.4 s for 3,000 sessions x 150 names with 6,718 fills. The `stocksense backtest` CLI and the real-data acceptance run are not part of this commit.

### D11
- `dens` (`models/dens.py`, key `dens`, trial family `g2-dens`) follows Zhang et al. 2020 / Qlib `DEnsembleModel` from memory of the paper, not from reading the reference code (no network use). Choices where the card left room: sub-model 0 uses all features and uniform weights; after sub-model k the loss curve is the squared error at `n_snapshots=6` cumulative tree counts (one incremental prediction pass); h = mean of the pct-rank of the ensemble-so-far squared error and the pct-rank of the loss-rank slope (last third minus first third of the normalised curve); 10 bins by h; weight = 1/(decay^k * mean_h(bin) + 0.1), rescaled to mean 1 and multiplied into the walk-forward sample weights. Daily realised vol `rv` = Garman-Klass (adjusted OHLC) over the full history, replaced by the 5-minute `intraday_rv` where a full 75-bar session exists (2024-09 on), floored at 0.2% a day (an unfloored ln RV has a far left tail from degenerate ranges). - 2026-11-20 G2-E2 (James-Stein decile map): `expected_return_map_decile(..., shrink="james_stein")` (default `"k"` = the C2 fixed-k shrinkage, unchanged) and `G1Config.er_map = "decile_js"` (spec key only when non-default, so every old hash is unchanged). Per date t the window's decile means m_j with sampling variance v_j = s_j^2 / n_j (s_j^2 the within-decile label variance over the window), mu = mean(m_j), tau^2 = max(0, var(m_j) - mean(v_j)), m*_j = mu + (1 - v_j/(v_j + tau^2)) (m_j - mu), then the same isotonic step weighted by n_j. Hand tests: tau^2 = 0 gives mu for every decile; v -> 0 gives m_j. Caveat recorded for the verdict: labels overlap (10-day horizon) and dates are cross-sectionally correlated, so n_j in v_j overstates the information; with ~25,000 rows per decile v_j is tiny and the shrinkage is mild, like the fixed-k rule (k = 200 against n_j ~ 25,000).
- 2026-11-20 G2-E3 (fractional Kelly): `S1Config.sizing in {score_over_vol (default), kelly}` and `kelly_c` (default 0.5). Decision on what "gross target" means for Kelly: `allocate_weights` rescales raw weights to sum to the room, which would cancel `c` entirely (c = 0.25 and 0.5 would give identical books). So under Kelly the room is `min(room, sum(raw))`: the book deploys c * er / sigma^2 per name and only reaches the volatility-targeted gross when the Kelly weights exceed it. With er in the 10-100 bp range and 10-day sigma of 5-8%, Kelly weights at c = 0.25-0.5 are large (each name ~0.05-0.3), so in practice both c values saturate the room and may give the same or nearly the same book; the grid reports that as the result.

- 2026-11-20 G2-E5 (OU mean reversion): `features/ou.py`. Residual `e = la - beta_{t-1} lm - industry mean of (la - beta lm)` (beta = 63-row regression slope, shifted one row; the industry term is 0 for UNKNOWN and for industries with < 5 members that day; the mean includes the stock itself). X = cumulative `e` over 60 rows, AR(1) by OLS (df = n - 2 for the residual variance), `kappa = -ln b * 252`, `m = a/(1-b)`, `sigma_eq = sqrt(var/(1-b^2))`; `ou_sscore = (X_t - m)/sigma_eq` only when 0 < b < 1 and half-life ln2/(-ln b) <= 30 sessions, `ou_halflife` whenever 0 < b < 1, `resid_rev_5 = -sum_5 e`. The zero-lag ADF is a simplification of statsmodels `coint` (which picks lags automatically) for speed; with a 252-row window the difference in the number of pairs found is small. ### M4 (2026-11-24)
Pure order-flow functions in `src/stocksense/orderflow/` (M4.1-M4.5, M4.7; M4.6 heatmap and M4.8 endpoints are separate cards). Every tool is an estimate from bars and says so. Interpretations where the card was silent, each deliberate:
- `vwap`: sigma is computed from running sums (`sum(w*tp^2)/sum(w) - vwap^2`, clamped at 0), which equals the card's `sum(w*(tp-vwap_t)^2)/sum(w)` with the running vwap. `anchored_vwap` does not reset at session boundaries and is NaN before the anchor.
- `profile`: `poc`, `vah`, `val` are prices: POC is the bin centre, VAH the upper edge of the top value-area bin, VAL the lower edge of the bottom one. `volume_profile` ties go to the lowest bin; `tpo_profile` POC is the highest-count bin nearest the range middle (ties lowest). Both take an optional `bin` (default `bin_size(mean close)`); `tpo_profile` uses the last session of the bars given. A bar touches a bin if the open interval of [low, high] overlaps it (a high exactly on an edge does not touch the next bin).
- `day_type` readings of the card's rules: double distribution = a run of >= 2 consecutive interior single-print bins with a >= 3-letter peak on each side (without the peak test every steady trend would be classed as a double distribution, as its single prints sit between two multi-letter bins); trend = range >= 2x IB range, extension on one side larger than the other, and close in that side's outer 20%; neutral = extension beyond the IB on both sides; normal = IB >= 85% of the range.
- `open_type`: "tests one side" means the first 15 minutes trade beyond the open on that side, and the > 0.3% drive must peak at or after the test extreme. `open_drive` needs every low (or high) not to cross the open.
- `delta_bvc`: sigma uses the previous `window` changes only (current change excluded), so the first non-zero delta is bar 51; a sigma below 1e-10 counts as zero (a constant step series otherwise gives sigma ~ 1e-17 and delta = +V).
- `divergence`: the lookback never crosses a session start (CVD resets daily, so comparing across days is meaningless).
- `footprint`: row `price` is the bin lower edge, `poc` is the same kind of price; display bars are session-aligned from 09:15. `stacked_imbalances(rows)` is public so the ladder rule is tested directly (BVC with zero sigma gives buy = sell, so a ladder cannot be built from bars alone). Imbalance rule: buy at a row >= 3x the sell of the row below (buy side), sell at a row >= 3x the buy of the row above (sell side); >= 3 adjacent rows.
- `big_bars`: baseline = median volume of the same minute-of-day over the 20 sessions strictly before the bar's day (null/skipped below `min_sessions`); the test is strict (`>`). `mfe` >= 0 and `mae` <= 0 are relative to the close in the bar's direction (`side = sign(close - open)`, a doji counts as up); all outcome fields are None unless the full window exists in `b`. The flag and baseline are past-only; outcome fields are the only forward-looking values and are labelled as such.
- `absorption`: "previous 20 bars" means up to 20 earlier bars of the same session, with at least 10 (so the first 10 bars of a day never flag; the median would otherwise mix in the previous day). A level is "hit" when it lies within 0.2% of [low, high]. When a lower (bid) and an upper (offer) level both match, the close in the upper/lower half of the bar decides.
- `timescales`: keys are "5m", "15m", "60m", "1d", "1w"; missing 15m/60m/1w are resampled (session-aligned from 09:15; Monday-based IST weeks). For 1d/1w the "previous value area" is the profile of the previous 20 bars. Missing inputs give fewer rows, and the score is the mean over the rows present.

Appendix A and the ANN run `timing(..., capital_inr=cap)` per capital; options are returns on underlying notional (the same at every capital), `net_2x = ret - cycle charges` (the statutory schedule doubled; no slippage exists in the option model), `gross` = 1 on days with P&L.
- Cross-sectional factors: scores -> `preds/<trial>/` -> `g2_eval.evaluate_model(base_id=s1-gbm-y_c2o_10-e0f7276025)` -> the `g2-s1` trial's `daily.parquet` becomes the candidate's series. That S1 backtest is its own registered trial (family `g2-s1`) and raises N in every DSR; its capital grid is the G1 grid (`settings.costs.grid`: 25,000, 100,000, 500,000, 2,500,000), not the library's. `k151_distress_voltgt` multiplies `net`, `net_2x` and `gross` by `voltgt_scale(net, 0.12)`. `evaluate_model` needs the feature store and a finished baseline GBM; untested against real data.
- Benchmark: `ew500_ret` for stock books, NIFTY 50 close/prev - 1 for m-index, 0 for m-opt. The family PBO (gate line 8) reads the inner join of the dates of every finished trial of the family, so a family that contains a short series (options from 2024-07, intraday from 2024-10) is judged on that short window.
- `eval.json` keys: `strategy`/`strat` (the library key, which `explore.gated_verdicts` and `picks` read), `trial_id`, `trend`, `verdict` (PASS only when all nine v2 lines pass), `lines_passed`, `gate` (`GateReport.to_dict()`), `net_sharpe`, `net_sharpe_2x`, `net_sharpe_by_capital`, `max_drawdown`, `per_year`, `chosen_exits`, `ic`, `trades`, `win`, `avg_R`. The daily series is `reports/m/strategies/<trial>/daily.parquet` (`date, ret, net, net_2x, benchmark, gross`), the file the family PBO reads.
### M5.9 (2026-11-25), Daily Picks (`strategies/picks.py`, `api/routes_picks.py`, `/api/picks`, `/api/picks/history`)
- Gated set: a strategy is gated when an `eval.json` of it has verdict PASS, `gate.kind` ending in `v2`, and all nine lines passed (the lines are read, not only the verdict; a v1 report never counts). Several passing trials of one strategy (intraday trend filters) collapse to the one with the best net Sharpe at 5 lakh. `w_s = DSR x min(1, trades / 100)` with `trades` = the trades of the whole walk-forward (`eval.json` `trades`), taken as `trades_oos` (every test fold is out of sample). m-swing and m-xs feed the daily list; m-intra and m-opt passes are listed under `other_gated` ("intraday/options" tab) with no symbols, because no live intraday or option signal source exists yet; m-index passes are not listed (the card does not place them).
- Signals: swing strategies from that day's scan parquet, state SIGNAL, at their default exit (the scan runs the default exit only, which may differ from the exit the walk-forward chose last); entry reference = the scan's `p_in` (the signal close), stop and target from the strongest firing strategy that has a stop. Cross-sectional strategies fire on the ten best scores of the u500 at `asof` (`spec.score(settings, asof, asof)`, injectable for tests); their entry reference is the adjusted close of `asof` and they have **no stop**: `Pick.stop` and `target` are `None` (the card types them `float`), the paper quantity is the equal-weight slice only, and they are not written to the journal (its exit rules need a stop). Gated picks with a stop are journalled as `picks:<best trial>` (`record_signals`, idempotent).
- Capital is an argument, read by the caller from the broker at decision time; never stored or returned (the file and the API carry `qty`, `notional_inr`, `risk_inr`). `build_picks(..., persist=True)` writes `data_store/lab/picks/<asof>.json` once; a second call returns the file unchanged unless `force`. The API serves the file; when it is missing it computes a preview with no capital and `persist=False` (nothing stored or journalled, so a preview can never block the real evening run).
- Exploratory list: SIGNAL rows of strategies that are not gated, ranked by the scan's full-universe `excess` per strategy (`<asof>_stats.json`, else by `rs63`), top ten, each with the label "EXPLORATORY — not validated"; a separate list, never merged.
- `picks_step(settings, state, clock, asof, capital_inr)` is the catch-up step for after "scan" and "flow_eod" and is **not wired** (no catch-up registry exists in this branch): idempotent by (job `picks`, date) through `State.job_*`; no scan file for the date gives `skipped`.
- `/api/picks/history`: past picks files with the journal outcome; `net_pct` = the move from the signal close to the exit price less the statutory delivery round trip on 100,000 INR (no slippage, the journal has no fills), `r_mult` = that net move over the initial risk fraction.
- The web page `/picks` of the card is not part of this task (backend only).
