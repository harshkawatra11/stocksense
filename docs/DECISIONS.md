## Phase 3

- T3.1 (2026-11-11): nodes are symbols (same symbol = same sid, so a symbol never maps to two sids and CA/symbol joins can use the symbol alone; `valid_from/valid_to` are informational). Edge B additionally skips a candidate B that already has an incoming official edge (a node may have one in-edge). Official edges are accepted closest-date-first, auto edges by `(last_date, symbol)`; a union-find rejects cycles. "Sessions between" is the difference of 0-based indices in the distinct `bhavcopy_eq` date list (all series). `sid_merges.csv` is appended to across runs; `rename_autodetect.csv` is rewritten each run. If an earlier auto-detected edge disappears later (a second candidate shows up), the two symbols keep their (shared) existing sid; sids are never re-numbered.
- T3.2 (2026-11-11): `compute_gap_factors(rows, ca)` takes `rows` = (sid, date, session_idx, open, close, prev_close) and `ca` with `sid` already joined (an optional `face_fallback` column feeds rights), and returns a SPARSE audit frame (only rows needing a factor, plus `ca_ignored` rows dated at the ex-date; extra columns `ca_types`, `note`); `build_daily_panel` joins it to the full rows (gap_factor 1.0, gap_src '' elsewhere). Every delivery or intraday BUY also leaves a cushion of (DP + minimum brokerage) x (1 + GST) = Rs 29.5 of cash, and a SELL whose costs would overdraw cash is rejected `cost_exceeds_cash`: without these a dust position (value below its own exit fees) drove cash negative, breaking the cash >= -0.01 invariant. An intraday position that cannot trade at the close (locked, no bar) is closed by force at the last close with fees and no slippage, reason `squareoff_forced`. Delisting write-off follows the card (20 sessions without a row and none later in the data -> 80% of the last close, no costs). Reference policies: BuyAndHold, EqualWeightUniverse (open of t+1; trades only names more than `band` off target), and the sleeve policies Clairvoyant / RandomPolicy (one sleeve of 1/h of equity per session: buy at the close of t+1, sell at the open of t+1+h). `labels/forward.py` does not exist on this branch, so `c2o_forward_labels(daily, sessions, h)` in engine.py computes the clairvoyant label from adj_close / adj_open. Truth test (synthetic market, planted_ic=0.10): Clairvoyant (k=5, h=5, Rs 25 lakh) net Sharpe 16.7; RandomPolicy (k=10) mean daily net return -17.5 bps against an expectation of -16.9 bps (1 SE 3.2 bps; the expectation is mean(sleeve fraction) x mean label minus the measured fee+slippage drag). Rs 5 lakh was too small for that test: with k=20 the Rs 5,000 positions pay about 2% round-trip in fixed fees and the book decays to nothing, which is the fixed-fee effect the card wants to see, not a leak. Engine runtime: 0.15 s for the 601-session synthetic market (40 names); 0.4 s for 3,000 sessions x 150 names with 6,718 fills. The `stocksense backtest` CLI and the real-data acceptance run are not part of this commit.
